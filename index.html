What We Specialize In ?

Complete Pathology Laboratory Setup & Support

We provide end-to-end pathology laboratory solutions — from planning and licensing through equipment supply, installation and ongoing technical support — to help you open and run a compliant, efficient diagnostic laboratory.

Complete Pathology Lab Setup

We establish fully functional pathology laboratories of all sizes—small, medium, and large—from initial planning to final handover, ensuring a smooth, coordinated, and hassle-free setup.

Floor Planning & Layout

We provide optimized 2D and 3D lab blueprints and workflow planning for efficient placement of work areas, instruments, and support facilities, while ensuring layouts are designed in accordance with applicable NABL guidelines, standards, and regulatory requirements.

Licensing & Regulatory Support

We assist with registrations, documentation, and regulatory compliance required to establish and operate a pathology laboratory, while helping you understand the NABL guidelines and requirements necessary for licensing and regulatory compliance.

Instruments & Equipment

We supply a comprehensive range of laboratory instruments, analyzers, apparatus, and consumables required for efficient and reliable day-to-day pathology laboratory operations.

Technicians & Skilled Staff

Provision of trained laboratory technicians and skilled personnel to operate and manage daily laboratory activities.

Technical & Ongoing Support

Continued technical assistance and maintenance services to keep laboratory operations running smoothly after setup.
